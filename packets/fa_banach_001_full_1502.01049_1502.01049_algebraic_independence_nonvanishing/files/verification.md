## Proof audit

1. There are exactly `2d^2` scalar entries in the pair `(zeta,xi)`.
2. A field generated by those entries and having transcendence degree `2d^2`
   forces the entries to be algebraically independent over `Q`.
3. Only finitely many algebraic coefficients occur in a finite family of
   polynomial functions, so they lie in a finite algebraic extension `K/Q`.
4. Algebraic extension of the base field preserves transcendence degree;
   therefore the same `2d^2` entries are algebraically independent over `K`.
5. The proof of source Theorem 4.1 establishes that, for a generic matricial
   polynomial, every scalar entry and its determinant are nonzero formal
   polynomials in those `2d^2` variables.
6. Their coefficients lie in `K`: matrix multiplication and determinant
   introduce only finite sums and products of the original algebraic
   coefficients.
7. Algebraic independence over `K` prevents any of these nonzero polynomials
   from evaluating to zero. This applies simultaneously to the finite family.

No numerical or symbolic computation is needed; the proof is purely
field-theoretic. There is no computational dependency.
