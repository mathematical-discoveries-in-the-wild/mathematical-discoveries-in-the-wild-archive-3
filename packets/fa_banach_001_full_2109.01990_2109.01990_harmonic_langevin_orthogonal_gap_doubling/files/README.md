## Source question

Yuanran Zhu, *A simple hypocoercivity analysis for the effective
Mori-Zwanzig equation*, arXiv:2109.01990, Section 5, open problem 1 on PDF
page 10, asks whether the Mori-Zwanzig orthogonal dynamics can have a
strictly larger spectral gap, and hence decay faster, for a degenerate
stochastic system.

The source writes this as `lambda_Q < lambda`, but its estimates use
`exp(-lambda t)`. Thus the displayed inequality conflicts with the prose:
for positive decay exponents, faster decay means `lambda_Q > lambda`. The
packet reports both conventions.

## Full result

For `gamma > 2`, consider the genuinely degenerate harmonic Langevin system

```text
dq_t = p_t dt,
dp_t = -q_t dt - gamma p_t dt + sqrt(2 gamma) dW_t
```

in `L^2` of its standard Gaussian invariant law, and let the rank-one Mori
projection be `P f = <f,q> q`. Put `Q=I-P`.

If

```text
a = (gamma - sqrt(gamma^2-4))/2,
```

then the optimal positive asymptotic `L^2` decay exponent of the original
semigroup on centered functions is exactly `a`, whereas that of the
orthogonal semigroup generated by `QKQ` on `QH` modulo constants is exactly
`2a`. Equivalently, the rightmost nonzero spectral edge moves strictly left
from `-a` to `-2a`. The fluctuation force for the observable `q` is exactly
`exp(-gamma t) p`, and its scalar memory kernel is exactly
`-exp(-gamma t)`.

Thus the orthogonal gap is not merely larger: it doubles, for every
overdamped friction `gamma > 2`, while the physically relevant force and
memory decay even faster at rate `gamma`.

## Proof mechanism

- The invariant measure is the standard Gaussian and the noise has rank one,
  so this is a degenerate stochastic system.
- Wick exponential vectors show directly that the Langevin semigroup on the
  `n`th Gaussian Wiener chaos is the `n`th symmetric tensor power of its
  two-dimensional first-chaos semigroup.
- The first-chaos eigenvalues are `-a` and `-b`, where
  `b=(gamma+sqrt(gamma^2-4))/2`, so the original sharp gap is `a`.
- The Mori projection removes `q`. On the remaining first-chaos line spanned
  by `p`, the compressed generator is `-gamma`. Every chaos of degree at
  least two is untouched. Its slowest surviving mode is therefore the square
  of the original slow mode and has exponent `2a`.
- The exact direct-sum norm identity is
  `||exp(tQKQ)|| = max(exp(-gamma t), ||exp(tK)|_{H_1}||^2)`.
