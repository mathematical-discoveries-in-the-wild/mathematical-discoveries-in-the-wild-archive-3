# Bounded novelty and literature audit

Audit date: 26 August 2026.

The run registry, solution index, attempts index, and result ledgers were
searched for arXiv:1406.1959 and the phrases `one-way LOCC`, `two-way LOCC`,
`unbounded gap`, `distinguishability norm`, `data locking`, and `subsystem
swap`. No prior run result was found.

Web searches covered the exact Section 7.3 question, the paper title and
citations, one-way versus two-way LOCC distinguishability, and asymptotic
local hypothesis testing. They found later separations in different
multi-copy or asymmetric-error regimes, but no primary source recording the
fixed-direction `Omega(sqrt(d))` norm gap proved here.

Novelty confidence is moderate. The collapse lemma is elementary, and the
asymptotic pair is already Theorem 3 of the source; the new observation is
that reversing the tensor factors turns that theorem into a full answer to
the later oriented question. The convention warning is essential.
