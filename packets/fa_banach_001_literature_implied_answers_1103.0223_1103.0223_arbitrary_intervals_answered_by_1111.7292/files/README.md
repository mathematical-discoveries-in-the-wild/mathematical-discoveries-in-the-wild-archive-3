# arXiv:1103.0223 - arbitrary intervals

Status: `literature_implied_answer`.

Austin asks in Section 4, PDF page 26, whether his continuous-time polynomial
multiple ergodic averages converge in L2 over arbitrary real intervals whose
lengths tend to infinity, without controlling their distance from the origin.

Zorin-Kranich's Theorem 1.1(1) in arXiv:1111.7292 (PDF page 2) proves L2 norm
convergence for polynomial actions of arbitrary locally compact amenable groups
along every Folner net, with a limit independent of the net.  Specializing the
parameter group to R and the nilpotent transformation group to the abelian image
of Austin's R^D-action answers the question.  Any sequence of real intervals
whose lengths tend to infinity is a Folner sequence.

The same theorem also answers Austin's higher-domain Question 4.1 and the
nilpotent-action extension in the polynomial (Leibman) setting.  It does not by
itself answer the intervening Hardy-field question.

The supporting authors cite Austin but do not explicitly label their theorem as
an answer to this Section 4 question, so the relation is recorded as an
agent-identified implication rather than `literature_already_answered`.
