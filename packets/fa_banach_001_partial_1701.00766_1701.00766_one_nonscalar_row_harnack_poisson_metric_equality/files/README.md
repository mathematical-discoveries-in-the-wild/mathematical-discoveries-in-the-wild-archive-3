# Harnack-Poisson metric equality with one nonscalar row

Status: likely valid partial result, pending human review.

This packet answers the open metric-coincidence question in arXiv:1701.00766
for every regular noncommutative polyball whose row-size vector has at most one
coordinate larger than one. In this class, Harnack domination and Poisson
domination agree with the same constant. Consequently their parts coincide
and `delta_H = delta_P` everywhere on those parts.

The proof combines two facts: the Poisson kernel is itself a positive free
pluriharmonic function, and the previously verified one-nonscalar-row
representation theorem says that every positive free pluriharmonic function
is a completely positive image of that kernel. Applying the completely
positive map preserves the defining kernel inequalities without changing the
constant.

The case of two or more nonscalar rows remains open. The durable PDF is
`solution_packet.pdf`; `verification.md` records the proof-critical checks and
bounded novelty search.
