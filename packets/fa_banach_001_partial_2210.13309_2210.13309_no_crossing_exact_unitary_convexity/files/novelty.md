# Bounded novelty and literature-status audit

Audit date: 2026-08-26.

## Source status

- arXiv:2210.13309 was published as Xavier Mootoo and Paul Skoufranis,
  *Joint majorization in continuous matrix algebras*, Journal of Operator
  Theory 92 (2024), 413-438.
- Published Question 7.2 asks whether continuous majorization of one
  continuously diagonalizable self-adjoint field implies membership in the
  exact finite convex hull of the unitary orbit.

## Searches performed

The audit used exact-title, exact-question-fragment, author/title citation, and
keyword searches including:

- `"Joint Majorization in Continuous Matrix Algebras" citations`
- `"continuously diagonalizable" "unitary orbit" majorization`
- `continuous Schur-Horn theorem C(X,M_n) convex hull unitary orbit`
- `Schur Horn moment map permutohedron toric variety section`
- `generic torus orbit closure flag variety weighted permutohedron`

Repository-wide cheap-index searches covered the arXiv id, title, `joint
majorization`, `continuous matrix algebras`, and `majorization`. No prior packet
or attempt for arXiv:2210.13309 was present.

## Findings

- No later arXiv paper found in the bounded searches states a decision of
  Question 7.2.
- The 2024 published source still presents the question as open.
- The supporting toric ingredients are standard but appear in literature with
  different aims: Lian records the generic torus orbit closure and its weighted
  permutohedral moment image; Sottile records the homeomorphism between the
  nonnegative part of a projective toric variety and its moment polytope.
- Searches found literature about closed convex hulls of unitary orbits, but no
  exact simple-spectrum result with `n` equal weights.

## Assessment

The theorem in this packet is a plausible new partial answer obtained by a
direct identification of standard toric geometry with the source question.
Novelty confidence is moderate rather than definitive: terminology varies
between operator algebra, Schur-Horn, symplectic, and toric-geometry sources.
The packet is therefore labeled candidate partial result pending human review.
