# Bounded novelty and literature audit

Audit date: 26 August 2026.

Searches covered:

- the run registry, solution index, attempts index, and result ledgers for
  arXiv:2210.13309 and the core terms `continuous majorization`, `Question
  7.2`, `single operator`, `unitary orbit`, `exact convex hull`,
  `unistochastic`, and `eigenvalue collision`;
- exact-question and exact-title web searches;
- citation/keyword searches for later work deciding Question 7.2;
- the 2026 revision of Chen--Li, arXiv:2407.00701, on continuity of the
  Schur--Horn mapping.

The source paper itself proves a related two-operator counterexample and
explicitly leaves the single-operator case open. The Chen--Li result is local
set-valued perturbation continuity for matrices with prescribed diagonal; it
does not construct a global fixed-weight finite ensemble of continuous
unitary conjugates. No later primary source deciding Question 7.2 was found in
the bounded search.

Novelty confidence is moderate-to-high, pending specialist review. The new
step is the two-direction scalar-collision thickening, which recovers the full
varying doubly stochastic obstruction from a single operator.
