# arXiv:1705.02623 — crossed-product amenability forces group amenability

## Outcome

Candidate full affirmative solution, with a stronger theorem.

Let `A` be a nonzero Banach algebra, let a discrete group `G` act on `A` by
isometric automorphisms, and form the twisted convolution algebra
`ell^1(G,A;alpha)`.  If this Banach algebra is amenable, then `G` is
amenable.  This answers the source question for every nontrivial
C*-dynamical system.

The proof makes an approximate diagonal asymptotically central under the
canonical group multipliers.  Its `(p,p^{-1})` coefficient masses then form,
after normalisation, a Reiter `P_1` net on `G`.
