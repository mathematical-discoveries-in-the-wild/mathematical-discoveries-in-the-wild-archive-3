# Fractional mass does not control flat filling in the weighted closure

**Status:** candidate scoped counterexample, likely valid, pending expert review.

**Source:** Marco Cicalese, Tim Heilmann, Andrea Kubin, Fumihiko Onoue, and
Marcello Ponsiglione, *A notion of s-fractional mass for 1-currents in higher
codimension*, arXiv:2403.03901, Conclusions, printed page 24.

## Result

For every `d >= 3` and `0 < s < 1`, there is a compactly supported closed
1-current `gamma` in the source space `C_s` such that:

- Corollary 5.3 of the source approximates `gamma` by weighted closed
  polygonal currents in exactly the weak-star plus `M_s` convergence proposed
  in the Conclusions;
- no 2-current of locally finite mass has boundary `gamma`.

Thus the closure generated by the paper's weighted polygonal approximation
does not have the requested filling property, and fractional mass does not
control the flat norm on that closure.  Already at the smooth diffuse level
there are cycles with fixed compact support and uniformly bounded `M_s` whose
least filling mass tends to infinity.

The construction is a lacunary sum of smooth oscillatory curls.  A mode at
frequency `N` with potential amplitude `A` has `C_s` norm at most
`C A N^{-(d-s-2)/2}`, while a compactly supported flat-dual test forces every
filling to have local mass at least `c A`.  Choosing summable `C_s` norms but
unbounded amplitudes yields one current with no locally finite-mass filling.

## Scope limitation

The source does not fix the coefficient group in its suggested definition of
"integer" currents.  Theorem 5.1 and Corollary 5.3 use weights `delta_n -> 0`,
which are generally noninteger.  This packet gives a full negative answer for
the closure actually generated by those weighted approximants.  It does not
settle the stricter variant in which every approximating polyhedral current
must have integer coefficients in the classical geometric-measure-theory
sense.
